Our Vision

  • We see ourselves placed with around 1000 keys across at least 10 cities in the next 2 years and gaining a large footprint across the country in key destinations attracting business and vacation travelers from different parts of the country and abroad.
  • We want to compete with the likes of the other luxury Indian hospitality companies such as Taj, Oberoi and global brands like Marriott,Hilton,Hyatt and much more.
  • We plan to develop a Royale Community with our members who come from different parts of the world
  • We also plan at a later stage of our business expansion strategy to expand our hotels at a global level to business and vacation destinations like London,New York and much more.
